THE FOUNDATION

Start with what matters most.

Brush consistently

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste. Use a gentle technique and replace your toothbrush regularly.

Clean between teeth

Use floss or an interdental cleaner daily. Ask your dental professional which approach fits your needs.

Keep dental visits

Routine dental appointments can help identify issues that home care alone may not address.
